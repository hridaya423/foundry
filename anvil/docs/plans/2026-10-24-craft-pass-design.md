# Craft pass design (2026-10-24)

Goal: raise the existing site to the Apple, Linear and Lusion bar through quality, not a new concept or new technology.

1. The real launcher at real size. One `Launcher` component that copies the Mac panel (750 x 495 pt, 26 pt radius, 20 pt search field, 38 pt rows with a fill and a border on selection, tracked caps section labels, footer keycaps). It is live: typing filters commands, math shows the calculator card, Enter opens that part of the story. The tools section uses a compact version of the same panel.
2. One tile material everywhere: the hero's glazed cream tile, lit the same way in tools, 81% and the closing arrow.
3. Type with range: four styles, display 120 px or more, section heads 72 to 96 px, body 18 px, labels 13 px or more.
4. One 12-column grid with 80 px margins, so every stop is a composed frame.
5. Motion with weight: tiles fly as a swarm, land with a small overshoot and settle in a stagger. One spring family.

Order: 1, 2, 3 and 4 together, 5. Capture and inspect frames after each step.
