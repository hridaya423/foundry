# Sound effect credits

Every recording placed by `tools/cues.py`. Kit sounds come from the sound-layer library (Kenney packs and Freesound, CC0); `audio/own/` files are cut by `tools/sources.sh`, and the typing takes are built by `tools/takes.py` from the kit keys below. Music and voice: see SOURCES.md.

- quindar-in: Generated: NASA Quindar tones are pure sines (2525 Hz intro, 2475 Hz outro, 250 ms)
- sfx-enter: Computed by tools/sfx.py (no recording)
- sfx-key-1: Computed by tools/sfx.py (no recording)
- sfx-key-2: Computed by tools/sfx.py (no recording)
