# Idea

> A machine for every job. / An app for every job. / One for every job.

Setup and payoff: the apps that pile up in Act II come back one by one in Act III, and one line in
Foundry's bar does each of their jobs. Armstrong's line spans the finale so "...for mankind." lands on the
wordmark.

# System

- **Three typists, one typeface** (IBM Plex Mono): a typewriter strikes line 1 (whole words, letters a
  little off the line, the tube jolts); an I-beam types line 2 in a note app; Foundry's molten caret types
  line 3 in the cast bar.
- **Past** in an amber tube on a Blender set; **present** full frame and crisp; heat is the only colour.
- **The wall**: the set tiled into an endless wall (`uBox`), other screens from a live 48-slot atlas.
- **Metal**: red = in the picture, blue = in the room (drips, the river), green = emission. The bar cools
  physically to cast iron, one band of light, then glass.

# Sound

Percussion and texture only: the machines themselves, a sub, and one breathing drone in B-flat. Every pad,
pluck and bell run is gone. The ending is the voice, the Quindar tone and silence.
