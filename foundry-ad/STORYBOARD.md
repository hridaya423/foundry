# Foundry — "Through the dot"

## The idea

One molten dot carries the whole film. It starts as the hottest point of a 1936 steel pour on an
old CRT, flies off a keycap as a spark, and from then on every scene **ends by flying into an
orange dot that is already part of it**, and the next scene is waiting inside that dot. The
camera never cuts; it keeps falling forward through dots. At the end the dot is the tittle of
the "i" in **find**: it pops off, the word becomes **found**, then **foundry**, and the dot lands
as the period.

Why this answers the references:

- **LexnLin**: one orange dot as the thread through every shot, one word per idea, letter morphs
  (ask → task, draft → craft). Here: find → found → foundry.
- **a16z**: the CRT open, the cut into a hard, fast edit, persistent element across scenes, the
  feeling of acceleration (scenes shrink from 3 beats to 2).
- **Lexis**: one world, one thread, then the product.
- **ideogram**: images inside type, type and image sharing one frame.

No UI mockups, no slides. Each Foundry ability is one verb, set huge, and an image that does the
verb.

## Rules

- Palette: ink `#060505`, bone `#F4F4F2`, molten `#FF5A1F` (with its heat ramp). Photos black and
  white; the only color is heat.
- Type: Archivo 800 at 75% width for verbs, IBM Plex Mono for the one-line readouts.
- Every transition is a zoom through a dot on the beat. Zoom scale is exponential in time
  (constant perceived speed), eased in and out, motion blurred by the 240 fps render.
- Every scene has exactly one idea and one moving image.

## Sound

- 0–1.875: room drone, CRT whine, projector flutter.
- 1.875: the strike (anvil, thock, sub), time freeze, the VO.
- 3.75–11.25: the drive. Every zoom has a whoosh that lands on the beat; each scene has one
  signature sound (typing, reel ticks, card riffle, orbit shimmer, seam clicks, pulse).
- 11.25: drop out, one tink when the tittle pops, a hit when the period lands, quench hiss under
  the cooling, a key click on ⌥ Space.
