# Storylines

The one thing: **Foundry does what you type, literally.** ⌘ Space, a few letters, Return, done.
The viewer's line afterwards: "the launcher where every answer is made of those tiles."

## A. Literal. (picked)

- **Device:** the feature as the hook; every command gets a literal, physical answer.
- **Form:** one frame that rebuilds. One wall of the wordmark's chalk tiles, never cut away from, and a seamless
  loop: the last frame is the first.
- **What you see and feel:** the real Foundry panel comes and goes over a wall of tiles, and the wall answers.
  "remove background": the background tiles fall off the wall. "24×18": 432 tiles step forward in a 24 by 18
  block. "make confetti": every tile is thrown into the air and rains back down as the wordmark.
- **First 2 s:** an orange, built from tiles, fills the frame in a raking light. ⌘ Space, the panel, "remove bg",
  Return. The panel is gone and the background tiles tip and fall away, row after row, clattering into the dark.
- **Beats:** remove background → 24 × 18 → translate → tile windows → download → make confetti (the drop) → the
  tiles land as "foundry." → line → the orange full stop opens back into frame 0.
- **Look:** ink #0f0d0c, chalk tiles #ece3d4 (the website's glazed tile), orange #ff6a2b only on the orange and
  the full stop; Geist; one raking key light, one soft fill, nothing else.
- **Sound:** the tiles are the instrument. Real ceramic, mahjong and domino recordings and one real keyboard, on
  one licensed minimal track. The drop is 3,000 tiles in the air.

## B. Never open.

- **Device:** life before and after. **Form:** split screen to one.
- Nine windows (converter sites, a downloader, a translator, a calculator) fight for the screen; each job is done
  once in its own app, badly. Then ⌘ Space, and the nine windows collapse into one panel that does all nine.
- Rejected: the machine / every-job films already told "an app for every job".

## C. The keystroke.

- **Device:** the object is the explanation. **Form:** an endless zoom.
- A macro of the ⌘ and Space keycaps; the press dives through the switch, the trace and the chip into the panel.
- Rejected: the latency hook was found unimpressive in launch film v3.
